How it works

Every device running Meshenger is a tiny relay. Messages hop from device to device until they reach the person you're talking to, even if they're out of your range.

STEP 1

Open it. No sign-up.

Your identity is a key made on your device. No phone number, no email, no account.

STEP 2

Find people nearby

Phones and laptops find each other over Bluetooth. Scan a QR code to add a contact.

STEP 3

Talk across the mesh

Messages are end-to-end encrypted and hop through other devices. Relays can't read them.

The honest version

What Meshenger protects, and what it doesn't. Yet.

Protected

  • Message contents: end-to-end encrypted (X25519 + AES-256-GCM) and signed (Ed25519)
  • No servers, accounts or phone numbers, so there's nothing to hack or subpoena
  • Relays only ever see ciphertext

Not protected

  • People nearby can see your nickname and public key (turn on Invisible mode to hide)
  • Relays can see which public keys a message is from and to
  • Global posts and your website are public by design
  • Not independently audited yet. Don't bet your safety on it

FAQ

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes. Turn on airplane mode, then turn Bluetooth back on. Meshenger never uses Wi-Fi, cellular data or any server.

How far does it reach?

One Bluetooth hop is roughly 10–50 m. Every other device running Meshenger extends that, so the more people around you use it, the further it reaches. Relays on a Raspberry Pi help fill the gaps.

What if my friend is out of range?

Your message waits and is delivered automatically when a path appears: they come back in range, or someone walks between you.

Does it work in the background?

Partly. iPhones and Android phones limit Bluetooth when apps are in the background, so keep Meshenger open for the best range. Laptops and Raspberry Pi relays run all the time.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

New apps from independent developers can trigger a warning until enough people have installed them. Downloads only ever come from this page and the GitHub releases page.
