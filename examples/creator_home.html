👋 Hey, I'm George — I built this.

Meshenger is a messenger that doesn't need the internet, cell towers, or anyone's servers. Phones, laptops, and little Raspberry Pi gateways hand encrypted messages to each other over Bluetooth until they reach the one person who can open them — anywhere people are.

And this page? It reached you the exact same way. There's no website and no hosting. My public key is the address, and the devices around you carried it here, hop by hop.

🛰️ How a message travels

🧑 you ............. 🔒 sealed
 │  📶 bluetooth
📱 a stranger's phone · ciphertext only
 │  📶
🛰️ a $15 pi gateway  · stores + forwards
 │  📶
💻 a laptop nearby   · passes it on
 │  📶
🧑 your friend ...... 🔓 opened
  1. Seal. Your device encrypts to your friend's key and signs it.
  2. Hop. Every device in range carries it a step closer — routes learn which way is downhill.
  3. Wait if needed. Nobody around? It waits in the mesh and moves the moment someone walks by.
  4. Ack. Only your friend can open it, and their signed receipt rides back: ✓✓ delivered.

⚡ What's inside

🔐 End-to-end, always
X25519 key agreement, AES-256-GCM, Ed25519 signatures. The devices in the middle only ever touch ciphertext.
🕸️ Gradient routing
Every packet teaches the mesh which neighbor leads where, so messages roll downhill instead of flooding.
📬 Store and forward
Friend out of range? Your message waits in the mesh and lands the moment they're back.
🛰️ $15 gateways
A Raspberry Pi on a windowsill extends everyone's range — and can even wake up a sleeping iPhone.
🌐 A web with no servers
global is the town square. Every public key is a website. No DNS, no hosting, nothing to take down.
👻 Invisible mode
Vanish from everyone's mesh map and keep your messages flowing.
📞 Calls, voicemail, view-once photos, files
Push-to-talk calls ring across the mesh. Files of any size stream peer-to-peer and pick up where they left off.

❓ Questions people ask me

Wait — how did this page get to me?

Devices carry it. I published it once; every Meshenger device and Pi gateway that met mine kept a copy and handed it to whoever came into range next. It's signed with my key, so nobody along the way can change a word.

Can the devices in the middle read my messages?

No. Messages are sealed to the recipient's key before they leave your device. Relays see an encrypted blob and a delivery address — that's it.

What if nobody's in range?

Your message waits, marked pending, and the mesh keeps retrying. If nobody picks it up for a while, the app tells you there's a gap in the mesh — plant a gateway there and it flows.

How do I make a page like this?

Open Network, tap the menu ⋮ → New page, or go to My site → Create home page. Pages are plain HTML: headings, links, colors, even images. No scripts, no trackers.
