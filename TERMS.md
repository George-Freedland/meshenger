# Meshenger Terms of Use

By using Meshenger you agree to these terms.

## No servers

Meshenger sends messages directly between devices. Nobody runs a server in the middle, so we can't read your messages, and we can't delete anything from other people's devices.

## Zero tolerance for abuse

Don't use Meshenger to send or publish:

- anything illegal, including content that sexually exploits or endangers children
- harassment, bullying, threats or hate against anyone
- sexual content shared without consent
- violent or extremist content
- other people's private information
- impersonation, spam, scams or malware

Identities reported and confirmed to break these rules are blocked for everyone in app updates.

## What you publish is public

Global posts, site posts and pages can be seen by everyone your mesh reaches, and they're copied from device to device. Deleting them may not remove every copy. You're responsible for what you send and publish.

## Block and report

You can block anyone and report people, posts, pages and sites from their menus. Reports go to the Meshenger team by email and are reviewed within 24 hours.

## Not for emergencies

Messages travel only as far as devices running Meshenger can carry them, so delivery isn't guaranteed. Don't rely on Meshenger for emergencies, or anywhere your safety depends on it.

## Age

You must be at least 13 years old to use Meshenger.
